Batch #16: Hydromel Strength Semi-Sweet Mead

Part of Experimentation Matrix 4

Procedures

  1. Sanitize all equipment:
    1. Primary fermenter
    2. Airlock
    3. Ladle for Honey Transfer
    4. Hydrometer and test jar
    5. 5 gallon water jug
    6. Stirring tool
  2. Ingredients:
    1. Build gradually, measuring gravity and temperature along the way.
    2. Measure 336 oz. of water and add to primary fermenter.
    3. Add 4.5 pounds of honey to primary fermenter and stir until fully dissolved.
  3. Rehydrate yeast.
  4. Pitch yeast into primary fermenter.
  5. Place airlock on primary fermenter and store in cool, dark place.
  6. Degas, record gravity and temperature every 12 hours for the first 36 hours.
  7. Record gravity and temperature every 24 hours thereafter until fermentation is complete.

June 14, 2026: Predicting 7.18% ABV at 1.000 SG.

  1. Assume 5 Weeks of aging before bottling.
